Membership questions

What is a booking window?

How far into the future your tier can reserve a spot. Starter members book 7 days out, Essential 14, Unlimited 30. The schedule always shows every class — anything past your window is simply marked as not yet open to you.

What happens when a class is full?

Members join the waitlist. When someone cancels, the spot goes to the first person in the queue — Essential and Unlimited members are placed ahead of everyone else. A credit is only spent if you are actually promoted into the class, and waitlist spots are held for members with classes remaining rather than sold as drop-ins.

What if I run out of classes?

Keep booking at your member rate — $22 on Starter, $20 on Essential, instead of the $28 drop-in price. Your allowance resets on your renewal date.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, and your class credit comes straight back if you cancel more than 12 hours before the start time. Inside 12 hours the credit is used — the spot can rarely be filled that late, and someone on the waitlist missed out.

Can I switch plans?

Any time. Choosing a new plan replaces the old one immediately and starts a fresh monthly cycle with a full allowance.
