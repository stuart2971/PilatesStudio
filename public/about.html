Our Studio

Strength, taught carefully

Core Pilates opened in 2018 with four reformers and a stubborn belief that people improve faster when someone is actually watching them move.

How we teach

Classes cap at ten on the reformers and sixteen on the mats. That number is not a marketing decision — it is the largest group one instructor can genuinely correct in fifty minutes. If a class looks full on the schedule, it is because the room is full, not because we are holding spots back.

Every instructor here holds a comprehensive certification and keeps teaching hours on the floor rather than behind a desk. Between them they cover rehabilitation, pre and postnatal, strength and conditioning, and classical repertoire. Tell them about a knee, a shoulder or a bad week and the sequence changes for you, quietly, without a fuss.

Memberships are monthly and cancel from your account in two clicks. We would rather you came back because the classes are good than because a contract said you had to.
